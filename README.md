## 📱 About PDF Wala

**PDF Wala** is an all-in-one PDF and image toolkit designed to make
everyday document tasks simple.

Whether you want to merge documents, split pages, compress a PDF,
convert images, scan documents, extract text, or protect your files, PDF
Wala provides the essential tools in one convenient app.

PDF Wala is designed with a clean and easy-to-use interface so you can
quickly find the tool you need and get your work done.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✨ Features

### 📄 PDF Tools

-   **Merge PDF** --- Combine multiple PDF files into one document.
-   **Split PDF** --- Split PDFs into separate files or selected page
    ranges.
-   **Compress PDF** --- Reduce PDF file size while maintaining a good
    balance between size and quality.
-   **Reorder Pages** --- Change the order of PDF pages easily.
-   **Rotate Pages** --- Rotate PDF pages to the correct orientation.
-   **Delete Pages** --- Remove unwanted pages from a PDF.
-   **Extract Pages** --- Create a new PDF from selected pages.
-   **Add Watermark** --- Add text or image watermarks to your
    documents.
-   **Add Page Numbers** --- Add page numbers to PDF documents.
-   **PDF Metadata** --- View and edit available PDF metadata.

### 🔄 PDF & Image Conversion

-   **Images to PDF** --- Create PDF documents from one or multiple
    images.
-   **PDF to Images** --- Convert PDF pages into image files.
-   **Image Compression** --- Reduce image file sizes.
-   **Image Resize** --- Change image dimensions easily.
-   **Image Conversion** --- Convert supported image formats.

### ✏️ Edit & Sign

-   **PDF Annotation** --- Highlight, draw and mark up PDF documents.
-   **Sign PDF** --- Add signatures to PDF documents.
-   **Fill Forms** --- Complete supported PDF forms.
-   **Flatten PDF** --- Make supported forms and annotations permanent.

### 🔐 Security & Privacy

-   **Password Protection** --- Protect PDF files with passwords.
-   **Unlock PDF** --- Remove PDF passwords when you have the valid
    password.
-   **Watermarks** --- Add an extra layer of document identification.
-   **On-device Processing** --- Supported operations are processed
    directly on your device.
-   **No Account Required** --- Use the app without creating an account.

### 🔎 OCR & Text

-   **OCR** --- Recognize text from supported scanned documents and
    images.
-   **Extract Text** --- Extract available text from PDF documents.

### 📷 Scan Documents

Use your phone camera to scan documents and create PDF files. Capture
documents quickly and keep them ready for storage or sharing.
